Game jam · 10th place

Infiltrate

Experimented on in an unknown lab: will you keep your humanity and escape, or take a heartless path of revenge as a cyborg?

Unity · James Cook University 5th Retro Inspired Game Jam, 8–10 Nov 2019

Overview

Infiltrate was made over a weekend at the 5th Retro Inspired Game Jam hosted by James Cook University Singapore. Our team built a retro-style, story-driven escape game with two paths: escape and keep your humanity, or turn cyborg and take revenge. It placed 10th.

At the jam

The event also included a public lecture by Mike Pondsmith, creator of the Cyberpunk tabletop game.
